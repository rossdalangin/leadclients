# Cold Outreach Sequence: Selling the Business OS

This 3-email sequence is designed for agencies to sell the GrowthPress OS to local dental, legal, or solar businesses.

## Email 1: The "Systems Gap" Insight
**Subject:** Your website is missing a brain...
**Body:** Hi [Owner Name], I was looking at your site and noticed you're using a standard contact form. Most firms like yours lose 80% of their leads because they don't respond in under 5 minutes. We've built an AI 'Business Operating System' that qualifies your leads and books them into your calendar automatically. Would you be open to a 5-minute demo of the new GrowthPress system?

## Email 2: The "Competitor Arbitrage" Proof
**Subject:** How [Competitor Name] is losing leads to AI
**Body:** Hi [Owner Name], following up on my last note. We just implemented an AI triage system for a firm in [City] that reduced their response time from 4 hours to 40 seconds. Their bookings jumped by 35% in the first month. I'd love to show you the 'Strategic Brief' our AI generates for every new lead so your reps never go into a call blind.

## Email 3: The "Final Value" / Automation Audit
**Subject:** Can I send you an AI ROI audit?
**Body:** Hi [Owner Name], I haven't heard back, so I'll stop the emails. Before I go, I'd like to offer you a free 'AI Automation Audit' of your current site. We'll show you exactly where you're leaking leads and how much manual labor you could save by installing a unified OS. Let me know if you'd like the report.
