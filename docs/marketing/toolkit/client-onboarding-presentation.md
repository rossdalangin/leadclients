1. **Capture**: Visitors enter through high-converting quizzes.
2. **Qualify**: AI scores every lead based on urgency and intent.
3. **Notify**: You get instant alerts for 'Hot' leads.
4. **Close**: Use the Client Portal for proposals and document management.

- **Activity Feed**: Real-time audit log of everything happening in your business.

- **Staff-Aware Engine**: Clients book directly on your site based on your team's real-time availability.
