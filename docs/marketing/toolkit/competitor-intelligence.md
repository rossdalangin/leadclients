# Agency Competitor Intelligence Strategy

In high-ticket local markets (Law, Solar, Dental), your competitive edge comes from speed and AI-driven personalization. Use the GrowthPress OS to systematically outmaneuver competitors.

## 1. Speed-to-Lead Arbitrage
Most local firms take 4-24 hours to respond.
- **The Play**: Use GrowthPress AI Triage to respond in under 5 minutes.
- **The Metric**: Target a "Time to First Contact" of < 300 seconds.

## 2. Behavioral Personalization
Competitors send generic "Thanks for your inquiry" emails.
- **The Play**: Use the **AI Strategic Brief** in the CRM to mention the specific pain point the lead described in their intake quiz.
- **Example**: "I saw you mentioned an energy bill of $300+. Based on our Solar ROI Estimator, we can likely reduce that by 70% in Dallas."

## 3. High-Authority Content Gaps
Identify what competitors aren't talking about.
- **The Play**: Use the **AI Content Studio** with the "Market Insights" tool.
- **Action**: Look for low-volume, high-intent keywords like "Emergency Dental for Children" or "Post-Storm Roof Claim Denials" and generate Authority Articles in the Knowledge Base.

## 4. Frictionless Closing
Competitors use PDFs and bank transfers.
- **The Play**: Use the **Secure Client Portal** with one-click proposal acceptance and Stripe integration.
- **Benefit**: Reduce the "Decision to Kickoff" time by 80%.
