# Sales Battlecards: GrowthPress vs. The Market

Use these scripts when prospects compare GrowthPress OS to traditional agencies or general SaaS tools.

## 1. GrowthPress vs. Traditional Web Agencies
- **Competitor Claim**: "We build beautiful, custom WordPress sites."
- **Battlecard Play**: "Beauty is standard. But does your site have a 'Brain'? Traditional sites are static billboards. GrowthPress is an **Operating System** that automatically qualifies your leads using behavioral AI and manages your entire sales pipeline. A website is an expense; an OS is a revenue-generator."

## 2. GrowthPress vs. GoHighLevel (GHL)
- **Competitor Claim**: "GHL has better automation workflows."
- **Battlecard Play**: "GHL is a disconnected SaaS that 'owns' your data and has a generic feel. GrowthPress is **Deeply Integrated** into your WordPress site. Your SEO, your content, and your CRM live in one place, on your own server. Plus, our AI triage is tailored specifically for [Niche], not a one-size-fits-all builder."

## 3. GrowthPress vs. Salesforce/HubSpot
- **Competitor Claim**: "We need an enterprise-grade CRM."
- **Battlecard Play**: "Salesforce is built for Fortune 500s. GrowthPress is built for **Elite Service Firms**. We give you the same Kanban power and AI deal probability, but we integrate it directly with your [Niche] tools—like our Solar ROI Estimator or Legal Intake System. We remove the complexity so your team actually uses the tool."
