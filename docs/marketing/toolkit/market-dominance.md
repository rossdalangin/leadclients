# Market Dominance Strategy for High-Ticket Agencies

## 1. The "Free Audit" Entry Angle
Use the **AI Content Studio** to generate a "Market Angle of Attack" analysis for a prospect. Present this as a free growth audit. It shows you understand their competition better than they do.

## 2. Niche-Specific "Land and Expand"
1. Deploy the **Lead Capture** and **CRM** first to show immediate lead volume.
2. Upsell the **AI FAQ Assistant** and **SMS Automation** once they see the CRM value.
3. Finally, move them into the **Proposal/Quotation system** for full closure.

## 3. White-Label Monetization
- **Standard Setup:** $2,500 (Niche Wizard + Branding).
- **Automation Retainer:** $999/mo (AI Content generation + Workflow management).
- **Growth Partnership:** % of revenue tracked through the GrowthPress Transaction engine.

## 4. Local SEO Dominance
Leverage the **Multi-Location** system. Each branch gets its own landing page with branch-specific schema. Use the AI Blog generator to create local area authority content.
