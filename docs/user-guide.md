# GrowthPress User Guide

Welcome to GrowthPress, the AI-powered Business Operating System for WordPress. This guide will help you set up and scale your business using our automated tools.

## 1. Initial Setup
1. **Activate Theme & Plugin**: Ensure both the `GrowthPress` theme and `GrowthPress Core` plugin are active.
2. **Configure API Keys**: Go to **GrowthPress > Settings** and enter your OpenAI API key. This is required for all AI features.
3. **Select Your Niche**: In Settings, choose your specific industry (e.g., Dental, Solar, Law).

## 2. Industry Onboarding
On the main **GrowthPress Dashboard**, use the **Niche Setup Wizard**.
- Clicking "Launch OS" will automatically create your core pages (Home, Services, Contact).
- It will also generate industry-specific sample data (Treatments, Properties, or Projects) to show you how the system works.

## 3. Managing Leads & Sales
- **CRM Kanban**: Drag leads from "New" to "Qualified" to "Booked".
- **AI Scoring**: Look for the score on each lead card. A score of 80+ indicates a high-intent prospect.
*Tip: Higher urgency keywords like "emergency" automatically boost scores.*

## 4. AI Content Studio
Need a blog post or an ad?
1. Go to **GrowthPress > AI Content Studio**.
2. Select your asset type.
3. Enter a topic (e.g., "The benefits of solar in Florida").
4. Click "Generate" and copy the professional output to your site.

## 5. Appointment Booking
Use the shortcode `[gp_booking_form]` on any page to allow clients to schedule calls. You can manage these in the **Appointments** menu.
