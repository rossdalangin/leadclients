# GrowthPress User Guide

## Introduction: How the OS Works
Standard WordPress sites are static. GrowthPress is dynamic. It functions as a **Business Operating System** (BOS) by connecting your website's frontend (what the user sees) with an intelligent backend (what you use to run your business).

### The Lifecycle of a Lead
- **Entry**: Lead comes in via `[gp_lead_form]` or `[gp_quiz_lead_form]`.
- **Analysis**: AI scans the message for "Urgency" (score 1-10) and "Intent" (Ready to buy vs. Information gathering).
- **Automation**: System creates a **Task** for you and a **Portal** for the client.
- **Action**: You use the **Kanban Board** to move the lead through your sales process.

## Step-by-Step setup
1. **API Integration**: Enter your OpenAI secret key in Settings. This "wakes up" the AI brain.
2. **Niche Selection**: Choose your industry. This changes the AI's "persona" (e.g., from a Solar Expert to a Medical Assistant).
3. **Initialization**: Click "Launch OS" on the dashboard. This builds your Home, Services, and Contact pages automatically.
4. **Customization**: Use the WordPress Customizer to add your logo and brand colors.
